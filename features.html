Features

Every Turn Lab cube is designed to turn fast, feel smooth and look good on your desk.

Magnets

Small magnets inside each piece pull the layers into place, so turns stop exactly where they should. On the Pro and Noir, you can set the magnets to light, medium or strong.

Corner cutting

The cube can still turn when a layer is slightly out of line. This means fewer lock-ups during fast solves.

Materials

Strong, light ABS plastic with smooth inner pieces gives every Turn Lab cube a quiet, controlled feel.

Finishes

Core and Pro are stickerless, so the colours never peel. Noir has a matte black frosted finish that is only made in small batches.
