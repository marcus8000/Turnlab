Why magnetic?

A regular cube relies only on its plastic pieces to stay lined up. If you turn too far or not far enough, the cube catches and you lose time.

A magnetic cube has small magnets inside each piece. They pull every layer into place, so each turn stops exactly where it should. The result is a cube that feels stable, accurate and fast, whether you are learning your first method or chasing a new personal best.
